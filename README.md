## Now building: Brainmerge

<a href="https://brainmerge.vercel.app"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ruben4reall/brainmerge/main/docs/brand/readme-header.png" alt="Brainmerge" width="720"></a>

**Every Claude account you own, side by side, with one shared memory.**

Brainmerge is a native macOS app that turns each of your Claude accounts into an app of its own, with its own window, name and Dock icon. Your accounts share one memory of your projects, a folder of plain Markdown notes, or keep one each when work should stay at work. It only launches the official Claude app and Claude Code, never touches a password or a token, and makes no network call.

Swift 6 and SwiftUI, MIT licensed, tested in CI, for macOS 26 and later.
